# When Should a Virtual Patient Ask? RISP-Guided Proactive Information Seeking in Multi-Agent Patient Simulation

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18183) · First posted 2026-11-13 · Checked 2026-11-21. Paper and accessible artifact listings reviewed; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

The simulator uses perceived risk and knowledge gaps to initiate questions in nursing communication practice.

## Proactivity Signal

The virtual patient tracks what it still wants to know and decides when to bring up an unresolved concern without being prompted.

## Evaluation Setup

Expert judgments and nursing-student feedback assess plausibility and experience during simulated encounters.

## Key Limitations

This is patient-role simulation, not evidence of clinical utility or real-world patient support.

## Use For

Compare how systems recognize and resolve the need for a question.
