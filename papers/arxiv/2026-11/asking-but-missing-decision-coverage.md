# Asking but Missing: Decision Coverage in Natural-Language Data Querying

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23864) · First posted 2026-11-18 · Checked 2026-11-21. Paper and accessible artifact listings reviewed; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

A diagnostic protocol separates detecting underspecified choices, resolving them from evidence, and writing executable code.

## Proactivity Signal

Checks whether a model identifies the unresolved choices that actually change a data query, even when it already asks several questions.

## Evaluation Setup

The study compares twelve models on BIRD-Pandas and traces where decisions disappear across the three stages.

## Key Limitations

Uses an existing data-querying benchmark; not an always-on assistant or a new dataset release.

## Use For

Evaluate a specific proactive decision separately from general task performance.
