# Thinking in Streams: Proactive Spatial Reasoning with Vision-Language Models

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25865) · First posted 2026-11-19 · Checked 2026-11-21. Paper and accessible artifact listings reviewed; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

Causal geometry estimates enrich a streaming VLM's cached evidence so it can recognize spatial conditions across viewpoints.

## Proactivity Signal

Tests whether a model waits until a requested spatial condition is met as the camera moves, or correctly stays silent.

## Evaluation Setup

ProSpace-Bench spans nine object-, camera- and scene-level tasks, with a human comparison and fine-tuned model baselines.

## Key Limitations

The spatial condition is given in advance; it is not unsolicited assistance.

## Use For

Evaluate a specific proactive decision separately from general task performance.
