# EviBridge: Encoding Predictive Visual Evidence for Proactive Interaction

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20916) · First posted 2026-11-16 · Checked 2026-11-21. Paper and accessible artifact listings reviewed; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

Two interfaces feed causal predictive features from frozen V-JEPA 2 into a vision-language model for state and tool prediction.

## Proactivity Signal

From early handover cues, the model can prepare while gathering evidence, then proceed or cancel as the situation changes.

## Evaluation Setup

The paper introduces FuGIBench and compares wait, prepare, respond and cancel behavior against task-adapted native baselines.

## Key Limitations

A narrow video-mediated handover setup. It does not establish physical robot deployment or open-ended intent discovery.

## Use For

Compare the conditions that trigger intervention with those that justify waiting.
