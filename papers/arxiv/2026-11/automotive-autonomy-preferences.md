# Towards Informed Autonomy Design: Understanding the Interplay of Function Characteristics and Autonomy Preferences in Automotive Agents

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19474) · [Code](https://github.com/M-Colley/levels-of-autonomy-assistants) · First posted 2026-11-14 · Checked 2026-11-21. Paper and accessible artifact listings reviewed; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

The work connects properties of in-car functions with autonomy preferences to guide design-time choices about permission.

## Proactivity Signal

Studies which in-car functions people want the assistant to handle independently and which they want to control themselves.

## Evaluation Setup

An expert workshop, 126 survey respondents rating 14 functions, and a nine-expert qualitative prototype evaluation.

## Key Limitations

Design-time preferences do not demonstrate live driving assistance or reliable individual-level predictions.

## Use For

Design and evaluate the component or controls needed for calibrated initiative.
