# Controlling Proactive Multimodal AI: Separating Perception from Participation

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22431) · First posted 2026-11-17 · Checked 2026-11-21. Paper and accessible artifact listings reviewed; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

A desktop design probe separates sensing permissions from participation and lets users inspect why an intervention was withheld.

## Proactivity Signal

SpotMe can keep observing while the user independently limits it to silently watching, asking, or offering help.

## Evaluation Setup

A 14-person Wizard-of-Oz study informed the probe; 16 participants then experienced, inspected and configured it on a civic dashboard.

## Key Limitations

A design probe and bounded user studies, not evidence of long-term deployment or a general model improvement.

## Use For

Compare the conditions that trigger intervention with those that justify waiting.
