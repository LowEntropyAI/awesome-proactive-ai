# BLIP-Live: Balancing Timeliness & Firing Precision in Proactive Streaming Video Agents

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21553) · First posted 2026-11-16 · Checked 2026-11-21. Paper and accessible artifact listings reviewed; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

Human timing annotations, a streaming harness and PAUC-F target the trade-off between early useful answers and excessive firing.

## Proactivity Signal

Learns when live video evidence is sufficient to speak while penalizing firing too often or too early.

## Evaluation Setup

The work evaluates frozen harnesses and trained variants on four streaming benchmarks, and introduces BLIP-Live-780K.

## Key Limitations

Tests timing under specified objectives. The paper announces code, data and checkpoints; public artifacts were not verified.

## Use For

Compare the conditions that trigger intervention with those that justify waiting.
