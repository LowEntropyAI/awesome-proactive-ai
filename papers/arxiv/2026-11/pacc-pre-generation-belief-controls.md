# Shaping AI's Beliefs Before It Generates: Human Sense of Agency in Creative AI

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21405) · [Project](https://proactive-co-creator.github.io/) · [Demo](https://aistudio.google.com/apps/bundled/proactive_co_creator?showCode=true) · First posted 2026-11-16 · Checked 2026-11-21. Paper and accessible artifact listings reviewed; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

PaCC uses clarifying questions, belief graphs and editable media attributes to expose assumptions before a polished output anchors the user.

## Proactivity Signal

Before generating media, the interface surfaces uncertainties and editable assumptions so the user can redirect it.

## Evaluation Setup

A small single-arm design-probe study gathered reports about agency, control, ownership and authorship.

## Key Limitations

The study is exploratory and relies on self-reports. This is initiative inside a creative brief, not unsolicited generation.

## Use For

Compare how systems recognize and resolve the need for a question.
