# November 15 literature scan

Since the [November 6 scan](2026-11-06.md), the arXiv API returned 3,148 records across cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.CV, cs.HC, cs.RO, cs.MA, cs.LG, cs.IR, cs.SD and eess.AS for submissions from November 5, 19:00 UTC onward. The newest returned submission was November 12, 18:59 UTC; later submissions had not yet been announced. A keyword screen kept 71 records, all new to the bibliography. A phrase search across all categories found five more records, all signal-processing or networking uses of "proactive" or "full-duplex", and Hugging Face Daily Papers for November 6–14 surfaced no further candidates.

Fifteen candidates were read in full from their arXiv HTML, with every linked repository, model, dataset and project page opened; the remaining exclusions below were decided from their abstracts. No code was run and no archive was downloaded. Dates below are v1 submissions. Every entry is a preprint unless its arXiv record states otherwise.

## Added papers

| First posted | Work | Role | Scope and reason to include |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 12 | [MindMem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16223) | Supporting | Relationship-aware companion memory; its "proactive intentions" shape reply content, and the user always speaks first. Synthetic benchmark, LLM judge. |
| Nov 11 | [Duo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15131) | Supporting | Full-duplex audiovisual model choosing speak or wait every 0.71 s. Inference code and weights public; timing not evaluated. |
| Nov 11 | [MemInStream](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15640) | Supporting | Evidence-controlled streaming memory benchmark. Harness and data public. |
| Nov 10 | [Deep Personalization (Coco)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13457) | Core | A local desktop agent learns from implicit feedback when to offer help or keep observing. Simulated-persona benchmark plus a one-week, 10-person deployment without a control arm. |
| Nov 9 | [AutoBuddy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12393) | Core | Always-on agents find the user's owed tasks in a simulated workplace and execute them unasked. Deterministic grading; no release found. |
| Nov 9 | [A Life of Its Own](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12076) | Adjacent | Code analysis of 12 open-source companions and 10 interviews on who governs unrequested outreach and whether refusals bind. |
| Nov 9 | [Joi-Bench-Task](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12118) | Adjacent | When full-duplex voice agents report background results under request changes and interruptions. Requests are always supplied. |
| Nov 7 | [TimeTraveler](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10337) | Adjacent | Streaming QA that routes questions to past, present or future evidence and waits for "tell me when" conditions. NeurIPS 2026 per the arXiv record. |
| Nov 6 | [HomeLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08914) | Core | Alarm-woken home robot benchmark where unwanted interventions fail as surely as missed ones; 14 of 20 tasks have no resident request. Release announced. |
| Nov 6 | [DeepRadio](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09418) | Adjacent | Agent decides when to return control before hardware and RF actions; 20-person within-subject study. |

Three of the ten are Core. Coco is the clearest initiative paper in the window: its contribution is the help-or-observe decision and its personalisation. HomeLoop is labelled Core because most tasks start from a device alarm rather than a person's request; if alarm response is read as a standing request, it would be Adjacent. A Life of Its Own has no figures, so its card uses an editorial cover.

All ten have five-section evidence notes, attributed author figures where one exists, bilingual initiative notes and release checks dated November 15. The AutoBuddy figure is the paper's own SVG. The Joi-Bench-Task and HomeLoop images were downscaled to 2,000 px wide.

## Exclusions

|---|---|
| Omni-JEV | Five-way listen/start/stop controller at user speech endpoints; reacts to the user, and data and weights are withheld. |

| An LLM Is All You Need (word-level full-duplex) | Data-efficient full-duplex adaptation; floor management, not a decision to help. |
| Time and Space (STC) | Specifies a proactive trigger but implements and evaluates only retrieval. |
| Knowing When to Intervene | Oversight interventions on an agent, not assistance to a person. |
| Real-Time Short-Response Selection; Kezek; Robots That Talk Too Much | Latency masking, duplex data preparation and a short position paper on turn-taking. |
| Unprompted coordination through shared memory; Explaining and interrupting collective misalignment | Agent-to-agent coordination and alignment. |
| Proactive denoising for collaborative filtering; SIGMO | "Proactive" refers to recommender denoising and motion planning. |
